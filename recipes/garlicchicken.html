Garlic Chicken Thighs

Description

Boneless skinless chicken thighs are easy to find in the supermarket and a healthy option. This meal has minimal prep other than mincing garlic and goes with many other dishes, pictured here with some roasted carrots. You can have a meal ready within 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Boneless skinless chicken thigh, 200-300g
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 10g butter, unsalted
  • ½ cup chicken stock, sub with water
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Season chicken thighs on all sides with salt and pepper and generous amount of garlic powder.
  2. Heat pan on medium and add olive oil.
  3. Add chicken thighs to pan flat side down, press lightly for first few seconds where possible.
  4. After 5-10 minutes, when golden brown, flip chicken pieces.
  5. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  6. Add butter to pan and then garlic. Cook for 1 minute until garlic is golden.
  7. Add chicken stock to pan and turn heat up to high. Scrape up any brown bits stuck on pan.
  8. After 1-2 minutes, serve chicken with 1 tablespoon of sauce poured over.
