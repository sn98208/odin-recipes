Steak

Description

Using a good cut of steak and seasoning with garlic and fresh herbs, you can easily replicate the steakhouse experience at home. Serve with roast vegetables, salad, or rice.

Ingredients

  • Steak, porterhouse or scotch fillet
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • 3 cloves of garlic, peeled
  • Paprika
  • 25g butter, unsalted
  • 3 sprigs of rosemary OR 6 sprigs of thyme
  • 1 tablespoon canola oil, or other oil with high smoke point

Steps

  1. Pat steak dry on all sides.
  2. Heat cast iron on high heat until smoking. Add oil.
  3. Season steak with generous amount of salt, and garlic powder, paprika, and pepper.
  4. Add steak to pan, pressing lightly for 30 seconds.
  5. After 2 minutes, when steak is nicely browned, flip.
  6. After 1 minute, add butter, garlic, and herb to pan.
  7. Spoon the melted butter over the steak to baste it.
  8. Remove from heat after another 1-2 minutes.
  9. Rest for 5-10 minutes then serve.
