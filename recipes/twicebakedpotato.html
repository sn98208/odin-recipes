Twice Baked Potato

Description

A comforting main or side with customizable toppings.

Ingredients

  • Brushed potato
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Jalapeno, sliced
  • Butter, unsalted
  • Chives or green onion, sliced
  • Milk or heavy cream, if desired
  • Bacon, chopped
  • Bacon grease, or olive oil
  • Tomato, diced
  • Tasty or cheddar cheese, shredded

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180 C.
  2. Wash potatoes until clean and pat dry.
  3. Coat potatoes with oil, generous amount of salt, and garlic powder and pepper.
  4. Prick potato with fork 8 times.
  5. Cook potatoes in oven for about 40-60 minutes, until soft in center.
  6. Slice potato in half and scoop out filling into a bowl, leaving about a centimeter worth of potato on the skin.
  7. Add butter to filling and mash to break up large pieces.
  8. Add bacon, green onion, some cheese, salt and pepper to filling. Reserve some of the cheese for topping.
  9. Mash the filling to desired consistency. Add milk, cream, or butter if desired.
  10. Top with jalapenos and cheese and return to oven for about 15-20 minutes.
  11. Top with tomato, more green onion, sour cream, remaining bacon and serve.
